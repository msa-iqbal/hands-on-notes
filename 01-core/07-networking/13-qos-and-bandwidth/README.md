# QoS and Bandwidth

Quality of Service (QoS) is a set of networking techniques used to manage, control, and prioritize network traffic. It helps ensure that important applications receive the required network resources, especially when bandwidth is limited or network congestion occurs.

Bandwidth management controls how network resources are distributed among users, devices, applications, and services. It helps prevent individual users or applications from consuming excessive bandwidth and affecting the overall network experience.

QoS and bandwidth management are particularly important in enterprise networks, ISP environments, wireless networks, and networks carrying real-time applications such as VoIP, video conferencing, and online gaming.

# 🗁 Practical Applications

QoS and bandwidth management are commonly used in the following environments:

* **ISP Networks:** Distribute bandwidth among subscribers and service plans.
* **Enterprise Networks:** Prioritize business-critical applications.
* **VoIP Networks:** Reduce latency, jitter, and packet loss for voice traffic.
* **Wireless Networks:** Manage available bandwidth among connected clients.
* **Data Centers:** Control traffic flows between services and infrastructure.
* **Home Networks:** Prevent individual devices from consuming all available bandwidth.
