# Background Jobs

Practical, structured notes for running **asynchronous, scheduled, and long-running backend tasks**.

## Contents

|#|Topic|What You'll Learn|
|---|---|---|
|01|Job Queues|Job queues, producers, consumers, workers, job states, and queue-based processing|
|02|Cron Jobs|Cron syntax, scheduled tasks, recurring jobs, and cron-based automation|
|03|Workers|Worker processes, background workers, concurrency, job processing, and worker lifecycle|
|04|Retries|Retry strategies, backoff, retry limits, transient failures, and failed jobs|
|05|Scheduling|Job scheduling, delayed jobs, recurring jobs, scheduling strategies, and execution control|
|06|Idempotency|Idempotent jobs, duplicate execution, safe retries, deduplication, and idempotency patterns|

## Structure

The notes progress from **job queues → cron jobs → workers → retries → scheduling → idempotency**.

Each topic is separated into focused notes for easier learning, reference, and expansion.

## Focus

- Background job processing
- Job queues and workers
- Scheduled and recurring tasks
- Worker processes and concurrency
- Retry and backoff strategies
- Delayed and scheduled jobs
- Failed-job handling
- Idempotent job execution
- Duplicate prevention
- Reliable background processing

## Goal

> Learn background-job processing systematically, build reliable asynchronous workflows, and keep practical job-processing knowledge easy to find.
