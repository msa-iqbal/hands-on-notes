# High Availability

Practical, structured notes for understanding, designing, implementing, and maintaining **highly available cloud systems, redundant infrastructure, failover mechanisms, and resilient architectures**.

## Contents

|#|Topic|What You'll Learn|
|---|---|---|
|01|HA Fundamentals|High availability concepts, availability objectives, redundancy, and failure tolerance|
|02|Redundancy|Redundant systems, duplicate resources, fault tolerance, and elimination of single points of failure|
|03|Failover|Failover mechanisms, failure detection, recovery, and traffic or workload switching|
|04|Load Balancing|High-availability load balancing, traffic distribution, health checks, and service continuity|
|05|Multi-Zone|Multi-zone architectures, zone redundancy, workload distribution, and zone-level resilience|
|06|Multi-Region|Multi-region architectures, geographic redundancy, regional failover, and disaster resilience|
|07|HA Patterns|High-availability architecture patterns, resilient system design, and failure-handling strategies|

## Structure

The notes progress from **HA fundamentals → redundancy → failover → load balancing → multi-zone → multi-region → HA patterns**.

The first section establishes the fundamentals of high availability, including availability objectives, redundancy, and designing systems that can continue operating when individual components fail. Redundancy then focuses on duplicating critical resources and reducing single points of failure.

Failover covers how systems detect failures and transfer traffic or workloads to healthy resources. Load balancing builds on this by distributing traffic across multiple resources and using health checks to maintain service availability.

Multi-zone architecture focuses on protecting workloads against availability-zone failures, while multi-region architecture extends resilience across geographically separated cloud regions.

The final section brings these concepts together through reusable high-availability architecture patterns and strategies for designing resilient cloud systems.

Each topic is separated into focused notes for easier learning, reference, practical use, and expansion.
