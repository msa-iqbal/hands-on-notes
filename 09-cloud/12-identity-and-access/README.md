Practical, structured notes for understanding, managing, securing, and controlling **cloud identities, authentication, authorization, permissions, roles, policies, service accounts, RBAC, least privilege, and federated identity**.

## Structure

The notes progress from **IAM fundamentals → users and groups → roles → policies → service accounts → RBAC → least privilege → federated identity**.

The first section establishes the fundamentals of identity and access management, including authentication, authorization, identities, and access control. Users and groups then cover how identities are organized and managed.

Roles and policies focus on defining and assigning permissions to identities and workloads. Service accounts cover non-human identities used by applications, services, automation, and cloud workloads.

RBAC focuses on managing access through roles and permissions, while least privilege covers the principle of granting only the access required to perform a task.

The final section covers federated identity, external identity providers, and Single Sign-On for centralized identity and authentication across cloud environments.

Each topic is separated into focused notes for easier learning, reference, practical use, and expansion.
