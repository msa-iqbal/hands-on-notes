# Disaster Recovery

Practical, structured notes for understanding, planning, implementing, testing, and maintaining **disaster recovery, business continuity, backups, failover, recovery objectives, and resilient recovery strategies**.

## Structure

The notes progress from **DR fundamentals → backup → recovery → RPO and RTO → failover → business continuity → DR strategies**.

The first section establishes the fundamentals of disaster recovery and explains how recovery planning relates to business continuity. Backup then focuses on protecting data and workloads so they can be restored after failures or disruptive events.

Recovery covers the processes and procedures used to restore systems, applications, and data. RPO and RTO define important recovery requirements: RPO describes the amount of data loss that can be tolerated, while RTO describes the target time for restoring service.

Failover focuses on switching workloads or services to recovery environments when the primary environment becomes unavailable. Business continuity then addresses maintaining critical business operations during and after disruptive events.

The final section covers common disaster-recovery strategies, including backup and restore, pilot light, warm standby, and multi-site architectures, along with their operational characteristics and recovery approaches.

Each topic is separated into focused notes for easier learning, reference, practical use, and expansion.
