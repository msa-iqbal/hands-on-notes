## Structure

The glossary is organized by **technical domain** so terminology can be located quickly without mixing unrelated concepts. Each page provides concise definitions and context for terms commonly encountered throughout the repository.

The categories follow the major areas covered by the knowledge base, from general software engineering and programming through web development, backend, frontend, databases, DevOps, cloud, networking, security, and architecture.

These pages are intended for **quick lookup and clarification**, not deep technical explanations. Detailed concepts should remain in their appropriate primary categories, while glossary entries should provide concise definitions and useful distinctions.
