# Services

Practical, structured notes for understanding, managing, starting, discovering, and monitoring **operating system services and background processes**.

## Contents

|#|Topic|What You'll Learn|
|---|---|---|
|01|Service Basics|Services, background workloads, service managers, service configuration, and common use cases|
|02|Daemons|Daemons, background processes, service processes, daemon responsibilities, and execution models|
|03|Service Lifecycle|Service creation, startup, stopping, restarting, reloading, failure, and lifecycle states|
|04|Service Discovery|Service discovery, service registration, service names, endpoints, dependencies, and locating running services|
|05|Startup|System startup, boot-time services, startup ordering, dependencies, and automatic service initialization|
|06|Service Monitoring|Service status, health checks, logs, resource usage, failure detection, and service monitoring|

## Structure

The notes progress from **service fundamentals → daemons → service lifecycle → service discovery → startup and dependencies → service monitoring**.

The early topics establish what services are, how background processes operate, and how service managers control their lifecycle. Service discovery covers how systems identify services and locate their available endpoints and dependencies.

The later topics focus on service startup behavior, dependency ordering, health monitoring, logs, resource usage, and detecting service failures.

Each topic is separated into focused notes for easier learning, reference, troubleshooting, and expansion.
